# AI Security Research Landscape — 2026

This document maps current research relevant to AYORAI Shield: security of AI systems, security of agents, and AI-enabled cyber defense.

## United States
- **NIST:** adversarial ML taxonomy covering evasion, poisoning, privacy and misuse; AI RMF and critical-infrastructure work.
  - https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/adversarial-machine-learning-taxonomy-and-terminology-attacks-and-mitigations-0
- **Carnegie Mellon:** AISIRT for AI security incident response; CyLab Cyber Autonomy Initiative for autonomous AI-powered cyber defense.
  - https://www.sei.cmu.edu/projects/aisirt-ensures-the-safety-of-ai-systems/
  - https://cylab.cmu.edu/news/2026/03/23-cyber-autonomy-initiative.html
- **MIT CSAIL:** autonomous cyber defense and contextual defenses for agentic AI.
  - https://www.csail.mit.edu/research/adversarial-cyber-security
  - https://www.csail.mit.edu/event/securing-agentic-ai-contextual-security-and-beyond
- **Stanford:** authenticated delegation, authorization and accountability for AI agents; agent-based cybersecurity research.
  - https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/project/loyal-agents/authentication-for-ai-agents-privacy-and-security/
  - https://reglab.stanford.edu/publications/comparing-ai-agents-to-cybersecurity-professionals-in-real-world-penetration-testing/
- **UC Berkeley:** agentic-AI risk-management standards and AI security research.
  - https://cltc.berkeley.edu/program/ai-security-initiative/

## Europe
- **ETH Zürich:** AgentDojo for dynamic prompt-injection evaluation.
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352
- **Oxford:** AI cybersecurity across security of AI, AI for security and AI-enabled threats.
  - https://gcscc.web.ox.ac.uk/ai-security
- **Cambridge:** AI Agent Index emphasizing the gap between agent capability disclosure and empirical safety evidence.
  - https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/ai-agent-index-safety

## China
- **Tsinghua:** secure/robust AI, AI security, privacy-preserving applications and distributed LLM systems.
  - https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/Research/Research_Groups/xuwei_0709.htm

- **Nanjing University SecLab:** security of AI-powered software and intelligent agents, plus fuzzing and vulnerability mining.

## Shield thesis
The field's strongest capabilities are distributed across benchmarking, prompt-injection research, tool/MCP security, agent identity, incident response, autonomous cyber defense and governance. Shield's research goal is to integrate these into one experimentally testable runtime architecture without making unsupported superiority claims.
